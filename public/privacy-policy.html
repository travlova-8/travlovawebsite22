1. Who we are

Travlova ("Travlova," "we," "us," or "our") operates travlova.com (the "Site"), a travel metasearch engine. Travlova compares publicly available prices and offers for flights, accommodation ("Stays"), car rentals ("Cars") and short-trip packages ("Quick Escapes") across third-party travel providers and online travel agencies ("Partners"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Travlova does not sell flights, hotel rooms, or car rentals, does not process payments, and does not act as a travel agent. When you choose an offer and click "View Deal," "Book Now," or a similar button, you are redirected to the Partner's own website to complete your booking directly with them, under their own terms and privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Search inputs such as origin, destination, dates, number of travelers, and filter preferences.
  • Contact details if you email us, message us through Chat with Us, or sign up for Price Alerts.
  • Any information you voluntarily submit through a form, survey, or support request.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps.
  • Click data: when you select an offer, we record a click record before redirecting you — the offer ID, the Partner name, the displayed price, a generated click ID, timestamp, IP address, and user agent. This is how affiliate commissions are tracked and reconciled; see Section 4.
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address, used only to tailor currency and regional results.

2.3 Information we do not collect

Travlova never asks for, and does not process or store, payment card details, passport numbers, or booking confirmations. That information is entered directly on the Partner's website once you are redirected there.

3. How we use your information

  • To display accurate, relevant comparison results for your search.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site's performance and features.
  • To log and attribute clicks to the correct Partner, so that affiliate commissions are correctly credited to Travlova.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To send Price Alerts or respond to support requests you have opted into.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your search or click history to make automated decisions that produce legal effects concerning you.

4. Cookies, click IDs & tracking technologies

Travlova uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and click identifiers) for three purposes:

  • Essential: to keep the Site functional — remembering your currency, search filters, and session state.
  • Affiliate attribution: when you click through to a Partner, a unique click ID and our affiliate identifier are attached to the outbound link server-side. The Partner's own systems then use their own cookie (commonly active for 24–30 days, sometimes longer, depending on the Partner) to match a completed booking back to that click, so that Travlova can be credited with a commission.
  • Analytics: to understand aggregate traffic patterns and improve the Site. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not sold.
You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may prevent Price Alerts, saved searches, or accurate affiliate attribution from working correctly, but it will not prevent you from using the comparison and search features of the Site.

5. Affiliate disclosure & third-party links

Travlova participates in affiliate marketing programs with airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, and car rental companies (each, a "Partner"). This means that if you click through from Travlova to a Partner and complete a booking, Travlova may earn a commission from that Partner. This does not change the price you pay — Travlova is never paid by you, and the price shown to you is the same whether or not you arrived via an affiliate link.

Because Partner offers are prominently featured and Travlova is compensated when you book with them, our comparison — while based on real pricing data pulled from each Partner — should not be considered fully independent financial advice. We display the "Recommended" badge on the offer with the lowest total price we found among compared Partners at the time of your search.

Once you leave travlova.com, you are subject to the privacy policy and terms of the Partner site you land on. We encourage you to review those policies, as Travlova has no control over, and no responsibility for, the content, privacy practices, or security of third-party sites.

6. How we share information

  • With Partners: we share the minimum data necessary to attribute a click and enable your booking — this typically means the click ID and our affiliate ID, not your personal search history.
  • With service providers: hosting, analytics, and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Travlova, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Travlova is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Data retention & security

We retain click and search records for as long as reasonably necessary to reconcile affiliate commissions with our Partners and to satisfy accounting and legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including analytics.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications, such as Price Alerts, at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. International visitors

Travlova may process and store information on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the Site, you understand that your information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries with data protection laws that may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and where changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice on the Site.
